Le Gong

Mathematics

About

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Vanderbilt University in 2026 under the supervision of Professor Akram Aldroubi. I am currently a tenure-track faculty member at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT).

Research

My research interests include applied and computational harmonic analysis, sampling theory, signal processing, and inverse problems.
